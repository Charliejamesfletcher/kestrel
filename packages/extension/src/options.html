What Kestrel can see

  • Only chess.com and lichess.org pages. It shows a Scout button on player profiles, nowhere else.
  • The addresses of your Chess.com and Lichess tabs, only to lock itself while a game is open.
  • It never reads your games, your moves or your password, and never runs on a game page.
  • Reports open on the Kestrel site in a new tab, built from public games.
